# Refund Policy

## Refund Amount by Reason Type

Refund amounts vary based on the reason for the return request:

### DAMAGED_OR_DEFECTIVE

- **Full refund**: 100% of the item price
- **Shipping costs**: Full refund of original shipping charges
- **Processing time**: Refund processed within 5-7 business days after verification

### NO_LONGER_WANTED

- **Partial refund**: 80% of the item price
- **Restocking fee**: 20% deduction applies
- **Shipping costs**: Original shipping charges are not refundable
- **Processing time**: Refund processed within 7-10 business days

### WRONG_ITEM

- **Full refund**: 100% of the item price
- **Shipping costs**: Full refund of original shipping charges
- **Return shipping**: Covered by the company
- **Processing time**: Refund processed within 5-7 business days

### OTHER

- **Refund eligibility**: Case-by-case evaluation
- **Standard policy**: May not be eligible for refund if reason does not meet policy requirements
- **Alternative solutions**: Customer service may offer store credit or exchange options

## Refund Processing

Refunds are processed to the original payment method used for the purchase. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and bank processing times.

## Non-Refundable Charges

The following charges are typically not refundable:

- Original shipping fees (except for damaged/defective or wrong item cases)
- Restocking fees (for NO_LONGER_WANTED returns)
- Customization fees (for personalized items)
