# Frequently Asked Questions

## What should I do if I receive a damaged item?

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

1. **Contact customer service immediately** - Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery
2. **Provide evidence** - Take photos of the damage or defect
3. **Full refund eligibility** - You are eligible for a full refund (100% of item price + shipping)
4. **Return process** - We will provide a prepaid return label for your convenience

## Can I get a refund if I exceeded the return time window?

**Standard policy**: Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery. If you have exceeded this window, standard returns are not eligible.

**Exceptions**: 
- **Damaged or defective items**: May still be eligible for refund or replacement even after 30 days, subject to verification
- **Special circumstances**: Contact customer service for case-by-case evaluation

## Are shipping costs refundable?

Shipping cost refundability depends on the return reason:

- **DAMAGED_OR_DEFECTIVE**: Full shipping refund
- **WRONG_ITEM**: Full shipping refund
- **NO_LONGER_WANTED**: Shipping costs are not refundable
- **OTHER**: Case-by-case evaluation

## How long does it take to process a refund?

Refund processing times vary by reason type:

- **DAMAGED_OR_DEFECTIVE**: 5-7 business days after item verification
- **NO_LONGER_WANTED**: 7-10 business days after item receipt
- **WRONG_ITEM**: 5-7 business days after item verification

Note: Additional time may be required for bank processing after the refund is issued.
