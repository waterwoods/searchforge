# Product Positioning Spec — Unified Intake (Narrow)

## Primary positioning statement

**Unified Intake is a paste-to-structure intake and handoff workbench for small California auto insurance offices:** it turns messy customer messages (WeChat/email text the staff already copied) into a **structured case card**—category, urgency, what was collected, what is still needed, a **single suggested next office move**, and an **editable client reply draft**. Nothing sends automatically; humans keep control.

## Alternative phrasings (2–3)

1. **“Front-door triage + case sheet for the broker desk.”** Emphasizes the first touch and the handoff artifact, not “AI agent.”
2. **“Message → next step + draft.”** Shortest; use when the audience already trusts you.
3. **“Office intake organizer for high-frequency auto insurance requests.”** Emphasizes frequency (add-car, billing, documents) without CRM or carrier claims.

## What we are

- A **unified paste surface** for inbound customer text.
- A **classification + structuring layer** (issue category, urgency, workflow hints such as collection vs handoff-ready).
- A **broker workbench** in the UI for queue, case focus, follow-up memory, and drafts (see `docs/STANDARD_SCENARIO_PACKAGE.md`).
- A **config-driven** product shape: industry pack + **client pack** for voice and handoff strings (same-industry replication path).

## What we are not

- Not a **full insurance automation platform** or autonomous agent.
- Not **CRM replacement**, policy administration, or carrier-connected execution.
- Not **OCR**, inbox connectors, or auto-outbound messaging (explicitly deferred in boundaries docs).
- Not **multi-tenant SaaS** or billing product in v1 (pilot can still be manual invoice).

## One-sentence founder pitch

“We help a small office turn every messy customer message into a clear case card—what it is, how urgent, what to do next, and a draft reply—so nothing important gets lost between WeChat and the broker’s desk.”

## One-paragraph customer-facing explanation

When customers text or email, your team often re-reads threads, re-asks for information, and loses urgency in the shuffle. Unified Intake gives you **one place to paste** what the customer said. It **organizes** the request, **highlights** time-sensitive issues, **lists** what you already have and what you still need, and **suggests** one practical next step for the office plus a **ready-to-edit** reply back to the client. Your broker or assistant **reviews everything** before anything goes out. It does not replace your systems of record; it **speeds up the front of the house** where messages first arrive.
