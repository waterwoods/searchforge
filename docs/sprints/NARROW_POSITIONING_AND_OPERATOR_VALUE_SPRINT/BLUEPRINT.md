# Blueprint — NARROW_POSITIONING_AND_OPERATOR_VALUE_SPRINT

## Purpose

Lock a **narrow, believable, sellable** definition of Unified Intake so frontend, hot-plug, pricing, and founder demos do not drift toward “full insurance automation platform.”

## Constraints (explicit)

This sprint produces **documents only**. It does **not** authorize:

- New architecture, OCR, carrier APIs, multi-tenant platform, full-office automation, large UI redesign, framework replacement, or broad feature expansion.

## Working loops (repeatable)

1. **Audit** — What does the repo + recent sprints prove the system does today?
2. **Positioning draft** — One primary statement + alternatives; test against believability.
3. **Operator-value framing** — Who saves time, how, without claiming end-to-end automation.
4. **Scope boundary** — In / out / later / never; protect the sale from over-promise.
5. **Monetization framing** — Small office, monthly pilot, what they pay for.
6. **Hot-plug fit** — Replication enabler, not the customer headline.
7. **Summarize → refine** — Founder-readable; cut anything that sounds like a platform pitch.

## Default hypothesis (to sharpen, not blindly adopt)

> A narrow, high-frequency **customer unified intake + case organization + office handoff** tool for small insurance offices.

## Success criteria

After this sprint, a founder can answer in one breath:

- What we sell **now**
- Who buys first
- What pain we solve **without** pretending to run the office
- What we **do not** claim yet
- Why monthly fees are plausible at small-business scale
- How hot-plug supports **same-industry** replication, not cross-industry empire-building
