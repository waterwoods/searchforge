# Founder Inspection Notes

**Purpose:** What founder should inspect after this sprint.

---

## What to Inspect

1. **Strongest scenarios** — Which flows held up under realistic stress
2. **Most embarrassing failures** — Where product would break in live trial
3. **Fix-now queue** — Top 3–5 items to address before trial
4. **Whether product is safer** — Confidence delta vs before sprint

---

## Which Failures Matter Most

- **Broker extra follow-up** — High (wastes broker time)
- **Customer confusion** — High (trust loss)
- **Wrong urgency** — High (cancellation risk missed)
- **Generic fallback** — Medium (feels robotic)
- **Handoff one turn late** — Low (acceptable friction)

---

## How to Use Fix-Now Queue

1. Review fix-now items with evidence
2. Decide which to fix before trial
3. Defer fix-next to post-trial
4. Use defer list for roadmap
