# Founder Demo SOP + Real Trial Feedback — Sprint Blueprint

**Sprint:** Founder Demo SOP + Real Trial Feedback Sprint  
**Created:** 2026-03-14  
**Scope:** SearchForge → Chen Kui Insurance Unified Entry

---

## Why This Sprint Matters Now

The product is technically ready enough. The gap is **operational readiness**: a clear, repeatable demo path and trial flow that Andy can run confidently and that a small-client prospect (e.g., 陈魁) can understand and trust.

Without a demo SOP and trial feedback loop, the founder risks:
- Ad-hoc demo paths that under-show or overclaim
- No structured way to capture what the prospect actually values
- Trial-blocking friction that goes unnoticed until too late

---

## Why Demo SOP and Real-Trial Flow Matter Commercially

- **Demo SOP** → Andy can run the same strong path every time; less improvisation, more consistency.
- **Trial flow** → Chen Kui sees: messy message → structured case → broker next move → human confirmation. That is the product story.
- **Feedback capture** → Learn what he values, what he distrusts, what he would pay for first.

---

## What "Good Enough for Founder Demo / Early Pilot" Means

| Criterion | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demo-ready** | One URL, one path, 3 strongest scenarios; Andy knows what to say first and what to avoid. |
| **Trial-ready** | Prospect can see the full flow (Customer Entry → Broker Workbench) and answer "Which scenario felt most useful?" |
| **Feedback-ready** | 3–5 questions asked after trial; answers inform next step (pilot offer vs. gap fix). |

---

## In Scope

- Demo SOP (pre-demo, what to say, what to show, fallbacks)
- Strongest demo path (primary + backup)
- Top 3 scenarios and sequence
- 3–5 feedback capture questions
- Fix only top 1–3 high-value, low-risk issues (wording, clarity, ordering)

---

## Out of Scope

- New feature scope
- Overengineering or generic polish
- Inbox sync, OCR, CRM, multi-tenant, Stripe
- Broad refactors
