# Layout Density + Professional Screen Utilization Blueprint

## Why this sprint matters now

Logic and triage behavior are ahead of **visual system maturity**. Sparse, centered columns on wide monitors read as “prototype,” which weakens **trust**, **broker confidence**, and **commercial readiness**.

## Commercialization angle

Buyers do not evaluate only correctness—they infer product quality from **containment, hierarchy, and screen usage**. Empty margins and floating cards signal “unfinished.”

## What this sprint improves

- **Structure**: Page shell, portal framing (customer), operational columns (workbench).
- **Density**: Workbench uses horizontal space for queue + workflow; customer stays calmer but less “lost in gray.”
- **Hierarchy**: Clear product chrome, section boundaries, module grouping.

## What this sprint intentionally does not do

- No new backend APIs, OCR, carrier integration, or demo queue logic changes.
- No animation-heavy marketing polish.
- No unification of customer vs workbench density (they **diverge** by design).
