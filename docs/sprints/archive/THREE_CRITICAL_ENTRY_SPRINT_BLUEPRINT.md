# Three Critical Entry Scenarios — Sprint Blueprint

**Sprint:** Three Critical Entry Scenarios Validation Sprint  
**Target:** Chen Kui Insurance Unified Entry  
**Budget:** 20–40 minutes

---

## Why These 3 Scenarios Matter Most

| Scenario | Commercial importance | Founder risk |
|----------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Payment problem** | Lapse risk = revenue loss; client panic; broker credibility | Demo shows "内容不够完整" → looks broken |
| **Quote problem** | New-car quote = high-intent lead; first impression | Generic fallback → loses lead |
| **Missing-doc / already-sent** | Client frustration; "why still chasing?" = trust erosion | Dismissive reply → escalates complaint |

---

## What Good Enough Looks Like

1. **Payment:** System recognizes payment/billing intent → gives payment-specific first reply → asks only next missing item (e.g. notice/screenshot) → does NOT say vague "内容不够完整".
2. **Quote:** System recognizes quote/new-car intent → explicitly says what it understood → asks only next missing field (e.g. ZIP) → does NOT sound generic or mechanical.
3. **Missing-doc:** System acknowledges first ("您说发过了…") → answers "why still chasing" if present → asks only next useful thing → does NOT sound dismissive.

---

## Non-Negotiable Principle

Evaluate like a real small-business customer. Do NOT just report "route correct." Judge actual reply quality.
