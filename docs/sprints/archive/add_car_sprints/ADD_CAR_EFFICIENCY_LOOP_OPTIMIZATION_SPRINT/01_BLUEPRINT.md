Strengthen the **Add-Car business loop** so it feels smoother, more trustworthy, and lower-friction: clearer step, completion conditions, progression, correction visibility, office readiness, and handoff clarity — **without** broadening product scope, redesigning the whole UI, or chasing generic visual polish.
