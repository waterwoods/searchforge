# Founder Trial Notes — Execution Sprint

**Sprint:** Founder / Broker Trial Execution Sprint  
**Created:** 2026-03-20

---

## 1. Inspect first (5 minutes)

1. **Customer tab:** Quick-start row + first message tone (Chinese client realism).  
2. **Workbench:** Sort/scan — can you spot cancellation vs add-car without opening?  
3. **Open one Add-Car case:** `quote_ready_status`, collected vs still needed, `broker_next_step`.  
4. **Append or follow-up:** Does the case feel like one thread?  
5. **Talk-to-agent:** Handoff immediacy and client reply quality.

---

## 2. Cases that matter most

- Add-car quote-ready **with and without** contact.  
- Missing document **already_sent**.  
- Payment / cancellation **with screenshot turn**.  
- Premium + remove vehicle.  
- Mixed-intent (add-car + doc question).

---

## 3. “Good enough for serious broker review”

- You can **hand the laptop to a broker** for 10 minutes and they do not hit a “this is fake” moment on the above cases.  
- You can **explain every awkward corner** in one sentence (“we don’t rate; office quotes”).  
- Automated guardrails **green**, so regressions are controlled.

---

## 4. What not to oversell

- No carrier automation, no guaranteed quote accuracy, no OCR.  
- Unified Intake is **routing + structure + drafts**, not replacement judgment.
