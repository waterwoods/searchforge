# Workbench Office Tool Professionalization Blueprint

**Sprint:** Workbench Office Tool Professionalization  
**Date:** 2026-03-20

---

## 1. Why Workbench Is Now the Next High-Value Commercialization Layer

The customer-facing intake is now much stronger:
- Add-Car is more realistic
- quote_ready_status exists
- contact identity exists
- attachment-ready-lite exists
- materials-sent hardening is live
- the customer entry page looks more professional

**The next commercial bottleneck is the office side:** When a broker opens Workbench, does it truly feel like a tool they can use to process real cases?

If Workbench still feels too internal, too lightweight, too easy to miss things, or too vague in next actions, then:
- paid-product feel drops
- broker trust drops
- real daily use becomes less likely
- the product still feels like a polished demo instead of an operational tool

---

## 2. Why This Matters After Customer Intake Strengthening

| Layer | Status | Next |
|-------|--------|------|
| Customer Entry | Strong | — |
| Add-car flow | Quote-ready, contact, attachment | — |
| **Workbench** | **Usable but demo-like** | **Office tool professionalization** |

The handoff is the moment the broker takes ownership. If it feels like a debug surface, the whole product feels unfinished.

---

## 3. What This Sprint Will Strengthen

1. **Broker scan speed** — Case understandable in 3–5 seconds
2. **Key signal visibility** — quote_ready, contact, attachment, correction, already_sent, urgency
3. **Follow-up visibility** — waiting_on, next_contact_by, due-state above the fold
4. **Case detail as working case sheet** — Stronger section hierarchy, clearer operational meaning
5. **broker_next_step** — Office-actionable, not vague
6. **Workbench as operational surface** — Not just debug/demo

---

## 4. What This Sprint Intentionally Will NOT Do

- OCR
- Carrier API
- Enterprise ticketing
- Multi-channel inbox
- Full CRM
- Large backend rewrites

---

## 5. Core Principle

**Do NOT add random new scope.**  
**Do NOT chase fancy features.**  
**Do NOT make the UI busier just to look "advanced."**

Optimize for: **scanability, actionability, trust, office usefulness, and commercial product feel.**

*End of blueprint*
