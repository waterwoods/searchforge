# Small-Business Readiness Stress Test Sprint — Blueprint

**Sprint:** Small-Business Readiness Stress Test  
**Date:** 2026-03-14  
**Type:** Evaluation-only (no feature building)

---

## 1. Why This Sprint Matters Now

The founder is preparing for Chen Kui trial and early pilot conversations. Real-world observations:

- **Turn 1 feels very slow:** 15–20+ seconds in real use
- **Language sometimes feels too system-like:** Not natural enough for small-business trust
- **Uncertainty on:** speed, smoothness, case report accuracy, sellability

Before committing to paid pilot or founder demo, we need a **brutally honest** assessment: Is this product strong enough to show or sell to a small business?

---

## 2. What "Small-Business Readiness" Means at This Stage

| Dimension | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Turn 1 responsiveness** | First reply arrives fast enough that a broker doesn’t think "it’s broken" |
| **Language naturalness** | Replies feel like a real office assistant, not a generic AI |
| **Case Report accuracy** | Collected/Still needed/Next step reflect the conversation correctly |
| **Trust / sellability** | A small business owner would try it and consider paying for a pilot |

---

## 3. What the Founder Is Worried About

1. **Speed:** 15–20 seconds feels too slow for Turn 1; will brokers abandon?
2. **Smoothness:** Does it still sound robotic or teaching-like?
3. **Case Report:** Is it accurate enough to act on? Would wrong output cause harm?
4. **Sellability:** Can we honestly say "good enough for pilot" or "demo only"?

---

## 4. What a Good Outcome Looks Like

- **Clear judgment:** Product is X-ready (internal demo / founder demo / pilot conversation / paid trial)
- **Concrete evidence:** What passed, what failed, what is inferred vs observed
- **Honest risks:** What would worry a small business; what is too risky
- **Actionable next step:** What to fix first; what can wait

---

## 5. What We Will NOT Do

- Broaden scope
- Build unrelated features
- Say "tests passed" without judgment
- Hide weak areas
