# Founder Inspection Notes

## Where to click

1. Unified Intake (customer entry) → **获取报价** (or equivalent quick start).
2. Complete a full Add-Car thread until **closure card** appears.
3. Scroll closure card top-to-bottom: **badge → headline → snapshot tags → office timing banner → 办公室回复摘要 → processing lines → boundary hint**.

## What “good” looks like

- Snapshot tags align with what you typed (year/model/zip/etc. as **field presence**, not full text dump).
- Timing line reads **business-realistic**; no “within 1 hour” style promises unless you deliberately change copy later.
- **提交加车请求** on first send when Add-Car intent selected.

## Regression watch

- Non–Add-Car handoff cards should **not** gain Add-Car-only panels.
- Workbench layout unchanged.

## Broker trial angle

Ask: “If I paid for this intake, would I trust the customer saw **what I see** in structure?” Snapshot is the customer-facing half of that story.
