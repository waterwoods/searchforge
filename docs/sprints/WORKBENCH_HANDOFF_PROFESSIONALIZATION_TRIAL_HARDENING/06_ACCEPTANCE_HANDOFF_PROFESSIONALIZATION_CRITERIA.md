# Acceptance / Handoff Professionalization Criteria

**Sprint:** Workbench Handoff Professionalization + Trial Hardening

---

## 1. Scan Speed

- Broker can understand case focus, quote-ready, contact, attachment, next step in **3–5 seconds**
- No need to read full conversation to understand "what matters"

---

## 2. Clarity

- broker_next_step feels like a **real office instruction**, not a vague summary
- Key signals (quote_ready, contact, attachment, correction, already_sent) are **visible above the fold**

---

## 3. Reduced Blind Spots

- Correction badge visible when follow_up_type=correction
- Already_sent badge visible when follow_up_type=already_sent
- Attachment count visible on queue cards
- Quote-ready visible on queue cards for add-car

---

## 4. Stronger Office Usability

- Workbench feels like a **working intake/case surface**, not a debug tool
- Next step is **actionable**
- Contact/attachment visibility reduces broker rework

---

## 5. Stronger Commercial Product Feel

- Professional visual hierarchy
- Clear "Your next move" block
- Key signals not buried

---

*End of criteria*
