# Acceptance / Simulation Criteria

**Purpose:** Practical criteria for sprint success.

---

## Realistic Coverage

- Pack has 10–30 conversations
- Covers: quote/add-car, material/already-sent, payment, renewal, remove-car, claim, Talk to Agent, mixed-intent, correction-heavy, vague/shorthand

---

## Weakness Discovery Quality

- Failures clearly documented
- Root cause identified where possible
- Commercial impact stated

---

## Prioritization Usefulness

- Fix-now / fix-next / defer clearly distinguished
- Fix-now items have evidence from simulation

---

## Small Fixes Justified

- Only 1–2 fixes if evidence supports
- Small, low-risk, high-value
- Directly tied to observed failure

---

## Acceptable to Defer

- Edge cases
- Broad refactors
- Out-of-scope items

---

*End of Acceptance Criteria*
