# Simulation Sprint Blueprint — Realistic Conversation Simulation + Fix-Now

**Sprint name:** Realistic Conversation Simulation + Fix-Now Sprint  
**Scope:** SearchForge → Chen Kui Insurance Unified Entry  
**Target budget:** 45–90 minutes

---

## 1. Why Realistic Conversation Simulation Matters Now

The product has:
- Stronger UI polish
- Stronger backbone (state, handoff, workbench)
- Stronger scenario package (64 inbox, 41 multi-turn, 27 adversarial, 23 complex)
- Stronger trial package and workbench handoff

**Gap:** All existing scenarios are relatively neat. Real broker trial will face:
- Shorthand, typos, mixed Chinese/English
- Vague wording, partial information
- "Already sent" / "already paid" frustration
- Correction mid-flow
- Emotional or impatient phrasing
- Talk to Agent mid-flow
- Mixed intent (2+ things at once)

**Why now:** Before real broker trial, the founder needs a reality check: Will the system hold up under messy, varied, real-world conversations?

---

## 2. Why This Is the Right Move After UI Polish

- UI polish improves first impression
- But if the conversation goes off-script, trust breaks quickly
- Realistic simulation exposes last weak spots before trial
- Reduces embarrassment during real use
- Creates evidence-based fix-now queue

---

## 3. What This Sprint Will Strengthen

- **Trial confidence:** Founder knows where it still breaks
- **Fix-now queue:** Prioritized by commercial impact
- **Root-cause map:** Why weaknesses exist
- **Optional 1–2 small fixes:** Only if clearly justified

---

## 4. What This Sprint Will NOT Do

- Broad new feature development
- Platform refactors
- Perfect the product
- Expand scope beyond Unified Intake / Customer Entry

---

## 5. Core Principle

**Do NOT try to fix everything.**  
Simulate → identify → prioritize → optionally fix 1–2 high-value items.

---

*End of Blueprint*
