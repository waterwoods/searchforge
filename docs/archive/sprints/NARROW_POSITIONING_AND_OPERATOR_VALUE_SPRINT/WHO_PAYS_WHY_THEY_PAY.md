# Who Pays / Why They Pay

## Who pays

- **Primary:** The **broker principal** (owner) for a **single location** pilot—simple economics, one decision-maker.
- **Champion:** **Assistant / office manager** who feels the paste-and-chase pain daily; they pull usage, owner pulls payment.

## Why they pay (value story)

| Pain | Unified Intake answer |
|------|------------------------|
| “Messages are messy and long” | Structured case card |
| “We ask for the same thing twice” | Collected vs still needed (when populated) |
| “I don’t know what to do first” | Urgency + one broker next step |
| “Writing WeChat replies takes forever” | Editable draft by scenario |
| “Things slip when I’m busy” | Workbench queue + follow-up hints |

## Why monthly (not one-time)

- **Habit product:** Value shows when **every** message runs through the same ritual.
- **Low friction:** Small monthly pilot fee matches **small business** cash flow; can stay manual payment until product hardens.

## What they are **not** paying for

- Carrier execution, compliance guarantees, unlimited lines of business, or “AI employees.”
