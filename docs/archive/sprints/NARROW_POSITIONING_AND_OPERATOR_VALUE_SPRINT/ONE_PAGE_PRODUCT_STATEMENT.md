# One-Page Product Statement — Unified Intake

**Name:** Unified Intake (with Broker Workbench)  
**For:** Small California auto insurance offices (often Chinese-speaking clientele)  
**Job:** Turn pasted customer messages into a **structured case** with urgency, **one next office step**, **collected vs still needed**, and an **editable client reply**—**human sends** everything.

**Flagship use:** High-frequency flows such as **add-car / quote intake**, plus billing risk, missing documents, renewal pressure, first-notice claim triage, and explicit human handoff.

**Not:** CRM, carrier automation, OCR, inbox sync, auto-send, multi-tenant platform.

**Why pay:** Less re-reading, fewer re-asks, cleaner handoff to the broker, better visibility on **same-day** risks.

**Replication:** Same CA auto engine + **client pack** (voice, handoff strings, UI copy)—hot-plug is **internal leverage**, not the headline.
