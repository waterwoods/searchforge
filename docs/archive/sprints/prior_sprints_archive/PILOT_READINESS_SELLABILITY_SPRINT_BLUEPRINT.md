# Pilot Readiness + Sellability Sprint — Blueprint

**Sprint:** 2026-03-14  
**Theme:** Product packaging for founder demo and small-client pilot conversation

---

## Why This Sprint Matters Now

The product is technically strong (speed routing, real customer pack, case handoff, trust boundaries, Simulation Assistant). The gap is **sellability**: a small-client broker may not immediately understand what this is, why it's safe to try, or what to expect. This sprint improves packaging—not features—so the product is easier to explain, trust, and demo.

---

## What "Pilot Readiness" Means at This Stage

- **Founder demo:** Andy can show Chen Kui in 5–10 minutes with clear "what this does" and "what it does not do"
- **Pilot conversation:** Chen Kui understands the value story and trust boundaries without technical explanation
- **Small-client trial:** A 1-person or 1-office broker can say "I get it, I'd try it" after a short walkthrough

---

## What "Sellable Enough for Small-Client Trial" Means

- **Clarity:** One-sentence value prop visible; "messy messages → structured case" is obvious
- **Trust:** "不自动发送" and human confirmation are visible and reassuring
- **Scope:** "What it does / does not do" is explicit—no overclaim, no confusion
- **Demo path:** Strongest scenarios (R1, SIM1–SIM3) are easy to find and run

---

## What Counts as Success

1. First-time visitor to Unified Intake understands what the tool is within 10 seconds
2. "不自动发送" and trust boundaries are visible without scrolling
3. Founder demo path (Load founder demo queue → Cancellation risk first) is obvious
4. No new feature scope; no overengineering; changes are small and high-value
