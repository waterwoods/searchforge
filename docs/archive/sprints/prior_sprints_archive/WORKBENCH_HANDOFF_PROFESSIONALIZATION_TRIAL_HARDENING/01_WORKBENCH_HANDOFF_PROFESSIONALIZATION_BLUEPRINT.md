# Workbench Handoff Professionalization Blueprint

**Sprint:** Workbench Handoff Professionalization + Trial Hardening  
**Date:** 2026-03-20

---

## 1. Why Workbench/Handoff Is the Next High-Value Layer

The customer-facing intake has been strengthened:
- Add-car has quote-ready visibility
- Contact identity exists (ADD_CAR_IDENTITY_CONTACT_LITE)
- Attachment-ready lite exists (ADD_CAR_ATTACHMENT_READY_LITE)
- UI is more professional (UNIFIED_INTAKE_UI_PROFESSIONALIZATION)

**The biggest commercial question now:** When the office opens the case, does it feel like a real working tool?

If the workbench still feels too light / too internal / too easy to miss things:
- Broker trust drops
- Follow-up quality drops
- The product still feels like a demo
- Paid adoption becomes harder

This sprint closes that gap.

---

## 2. Why This Matters After Customer-Entry and Add-Car Strengthening

| Layer | Status | Next |
|-------|--------|------|
| Customer Entry | Strong | — |
| Add-car flow | Quote-ready, contact, attachment | — |
| **Workbench handoff** | **Usable but demo-like** | **Professionalization** |

The handoff is the moment the broker takes ownership. If it feels like a debug surface, the whole product feels unfinished.

---

## 3. What This Sprint Will Strengthen

1. **Broker scan speed** — Case understandable in 3–5 seconds
2. **Key signal visibility** — quote_ready, contact, attachment, correction, already_sent, urgency
3. **broker_next_step** — Office-actionable, not vague
4. **Workbench as operational surface** — Not just debug/demo
5. **Trial hardening** — Realistic broker-side simulations

---

## 4. What This Sprint Intentionally Will NOT Do

- Full enterprise ticketing
- Multi-agent assignment
- SLA engine
- OCR
- Carrier integration
- Rewriting backend architecture
- Adding big new channels

---

## 5. Core Principle

**Do NOT overbuild.**  
**Do NOT add complexity just to look sophisticated.**  
**Do NOT lose the current strengths.**

This sprint is about: **clearer broker scanning, clearer next step, clearer case completeness, clearer signals.**
