# ADD-CAR HANDOFF + FLOW HARDENING FROM SCORECARD — Blueprint

## Sprint goal

Raise **HANDOFF** and **FLOW** quality on the **Add-Car flagship path** using the Industrial Scorecard V1 as the single source of truth—without page redesign or scope drift.

## Why now

Scorecard V1 rated **PAGE 4/5** but **HANDOFF 3/5** and **FLOW 3/5**. Pilot trust is decided **after submit**: whether the customer feels **received**, whether **office review** feels real, and whether **next steps** read as a **single business task** rather than more chat turns.

## Scorecard basis

- `docs/sprints/ADD_CAR_INDUSTRIAL_SCORECARD_V1_SPRINT/02_ADD_CAR_INDUSTRIAL_SCORECARD_V1.md`
- `docs/sprints/ADD_CAR_INDUSTRIAL_SCORECARD_V1_SPRINT/03_FINAL_REPORT.md`

## Scope

- Add-Car **customer-visible handoff lines** (config + minimal engine ack tone).
- Add-Car **already-sent / materials-sent** and **clarification follow-up** copy.
- **Same-case continuity** (append-boundary default for add-car).
- **Flow framing** on the portal: step labels + closure/processing lines that stress **division of labor** after submit.

## Non-scope

- Broad UI redesign, non–Add-Car flows, CRM/auth/billing, large engine refactors, heavy new docs.

## Target outcome

- Shorter, more **desk-like** acknowledgements and handoffs.
- Clearer **“you wait / office acts”** after materials sent or handoff.
- Slightly stronger **task progression** language (flow track + closure card) without changing core behavior.
