# ADD-CAR TASK-FIRST / LESS-CHAT FINAL POLISH — Blueprint

## Sprint goal

Nudge the Add-Car flagship flow one step from **chat-shaped intake with business structure** toward **task-first business flow with chat as input only**, without redesigning the page or rewriting the engine.

## Why now

`PROJECT_TRUTH_SWITCH.md` and the Add-Car industrial scorecard agree: **PAGE and handoff copy are relatively strong**; the remaining gap is **conversation-first gravity** (FLOW/HANDOFF perception). A short, bounded polish improves founder demo and pilot first impression before pushing usage harder.

## Scope

- Post-submit **hierarchy and wording**: task record primary, transcript secondary.
- **Office ownership** and **no-repeat** reassurance in configurable copy.
- **Small UI emphasis** only where it supports the above (e.g. section labels, typography deemphasis for closure body on Add-Car handoff).
- Lightweight validation and this three-file sprint folder.

## Non-scope

- Page redesign, new features, broad state-machine or backend logic changes, non–Add-Car expansion, heavy documentation.

## Remaining chat-first problem (before this sprint)

- Post-handoff area still mixed **thread chrome** (headings, collapse labels, “摘要给客户”) with **result card**, so the eye could still anchor on **bubbles and the big reply** instead of **record + next step + office**.

## Target outcome

After submit, the customer more clearly reads: **this is one service record with the office; the scrollable bubbles are optional audit trail; repeating what you already submitted is unnecessary.**
