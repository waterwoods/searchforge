# Add-Car Founder Trial Blueprint

**Sprint:** Add-Car Founder Trial + Hybrid Intake Hardening  
**Product:** SearchForge → Chen Kui Insurance Unified Entry  
**Date:** 2026-03-20

## Why Add-Car is the right flagship

- **Highest end-to-end depth:** Multi-turn collection, `quote_ready_status`, contact-lite, attachment-ready-lite, materials-sent and correction handling, workbench scanability — more “complete product” than most other intents.
- **Clearest paid-trial story:** Brokers immediately understand “collect vehicle + zip + delivery + driver → quote-ready handoff” as time saved vs. WeChat/email ping-pong.
- **Repeatable in a small office:** Same shape of work every week (new car, new household driver, timing).

## Why founder trial before broker trial

- Surfaces **demo seams** (stitched flows, chat-first friction, light case feel) without burning broker trust.
- Validates **intake mode** (chat vs form vs hybrid) against “would I use this Monday morning?” not “is the AI clever?”
- Produces a **fix-now list** aligned to commercial criteria: time saved, fewer repeated questions, clearer handoff.

## What this sprint evaluates

1. Realistic founder/broker-style Add-Car paths (normal, quote-ready gaps, materials sent, correction, side question, progressive fill, hesitation).
2. **Intake modes:** chat-only, form-first, hybrid — speed, trust, completeness, broker usefulness, implementation cost.
3. Highest-ROI **single improvement** to Add-Car customer entry and perceived office-readiness.

## What this sprint intentionally does not do

- OCR, carrier APIs, real rating engine, new unrelated scenarios, enterprise ticketing, full CRM, large architecture rewrites.

## Success shape

Founder can answer: “Does Add-Car save time and feel safe to show a broker?” with explicit gaps and one concrete hybrid intake behavior shipped or specified.
