**Created:** 2026-03-20

## What founder should inspect after this sprint

1. **Queue cards** — Open 办公室工作台 → 最近 case. Check readiness tags (可行动, 需更多信息, etc.) and compact preview line (已收集/还缺 in Chinese).
2. **Case detail** — Open any case. Check "已收集" / "还缺" field names (车型, 车架号, etc.), "您的下一步", "可审核草稿" / "需您修改后再发".
3. **Copy case snapshot** — Click "复制 case 摘要". Paste into a note. Verify labels: 案件, 下一步, 已收集, 还缺, 草稿.
4. **Case focus tags** — Ensure tags like 加车报价, 事故报险, 保费复查 appear instead of Add car quote, Claim intake, Premium review.
5. **Category display** — Cancellation risk, Missing document, etc. → 取消风险, 材料补交 (or equivalent).
6. **Overall tone** — Does Workbench feel more like an office tool and less like an internal prototype?

## Which areas matter most

- **Queue preview** (compact line under each card) — highest scan frequency
- **Queue readiness tag** — quick "can I act?" signal
- **Case detail field labels** — when broker digs in

## What "polished enough for broker demo" looks like

- No visible English in queue cards or case detail except where contextually appropriate (e.g. VIN, ZIP as industry terms)
- Consistent office tone: 已收集, 还缺, 下一步, 跟进, 在等
- Copy-paste output usable in Chinese-language notes/email
