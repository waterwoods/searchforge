# Broker Demo — What to Say (Simple Language)

Short phrases for each part of the demo. Use as-is or adapt.

---

## Opening

- "This is a California auto insurance assistant for brokers."
- "It answers common client questions using official sources — DMV, California Department of Insurance, and insurer sites."
- "You can ask in Chinese or English."

---

## When Showing a Question

- "A typical client question."
- "The system returns a concise answer plus official links you can send to the client."
- "You get steps and links to share."
- "Brokers can quickly verify and share with clients."

---

## When Showing 复制给客户

- "This copies a client-ready version you can paste into WeChat."
- "Format: question, bullets, steps, and official links."

---

## When Showing Citations

- "Each result shows the source — government or insurer."
- "GOV means official; INSURER means insurer site."
- "You can click through to the original page."

---

## If Switching to Offline

- "Let me switch to our offline demo mode."
- "We keep pre-saved answers for the most common questions."
- "The demo can run even when the live system is unavailable."

---

## Wrap-Up

- "The system is designed to reduce your lookup time."
- "You get authoritative answers you can share with clients."
- "We're iterating based on broker feedback."

---

## Ask for Feedback

- "What would make this most useful for your day-to-day work?"
- "Any questions or scenarios we should add?"
- "What's the one thing you'd want this to do first?"

---

## What to Avoid

- Don't say "AI" or "LLM" unless asked — focus on "assistant" and "official sources."
- Don't promise features not yet built (Stripe, auth, multi-tenant).
- Don't type custom questions in Offline mode — they will fail.
- Don't apologize for Offline mode — frame it as a deliberate fallback.
