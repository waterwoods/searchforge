## Customer First north star (P16 evolution)

### Umbrella principle — Customer Must Always Know The Status

The strongest Customer First principle is not a new constitution rule. It is a **named north star umbrella**:

> **Customer Must Always Know The Status**

The customer must always be able to answer these three questions **without calling the office**:

1. **Did I submit my request?** (submit state)
2. **What is still missing?** (missing fields)
3. **What is the current contact state?** (who is waiting on whom, and what happens next)

P16 is the **Customer First Add-Car Intake System**. Office and broker surfaces exist to make those three answers true on the customer surface — not to replace them with internal jargon or phone callbacks for basic status.
