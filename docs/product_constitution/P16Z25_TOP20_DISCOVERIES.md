# P16-Z2.5 Top 20 Discoveries

**Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Sprint:** P16-Z2.5 Product Soul Synthesis  
**Question:** What surprised us?

Ranked by surprise × strategic impact.

---

## 1. The valuable product already exists in the repository

**Surprise:** After 16+ sprints, archaeology found a broker-grade triage engine with append, case intelligence, OCR side paths, commercial docs, and guardrails — hidden behind SSO and undiscoverable UX.

**Implication:** Next sprint is revival, not invention.

---

## 2. Trial fails on a 5-minute founder toggle, not engineering

**Surprise:** FP-004 Preview SSO has blocked Chen Kui Day 0 across multiple sprints while engine scores climbed to 85+.

**Implication:** Founder ops is P0, not backlog.

---

## 3. Office Actionability was already at ceiling before P16-Y

**Surprise:** All 50 battery cases passed broker_next_step quality. Intelligence work was classification/extraction, not draft generation.

**Implication:** Don't rebuild the draft engine — tune classification and summary merge.

---

## 4. Backend append works; UI doesn't teach it

**Surprise:** Full `triage_for_append` API with boundary detection exists. Cap 5 scores 41/100 because UX gates append on queue reopen.

**Implication:** Highest-ROI fix is copy/layout, not API.

---

## 5. Product wins Turn 1, loses Turn 2+

**Surprise:** Role C simulation showed 43/100 cold score not from bad triage but from copy-as-exit-point behavior. Users treat product as spell-checker popup.

**Implication:** Continuity cues matter more than smarter first draft.

---

## 6. Local health ≠ deployed reality (FP-008)

**Surprise:** P16-J scored 74/100 locally; deployed broker cold score was 12–52/100. Guardrail PASS did not mean broker could reach engine.

**Implication:** Cold URL curl must gate every "shippable" claim.

---

## 7. Commercial pack complete but payment impossible

**Surprise:** P16-K closed with pricing, terms, invoices, Day 0/7 scripts — yet Andy cannot ask for money because invoice IDs empty and zero observation log rows.

**Implication:** Docs ≠ process execution.

---

## 8. Customer mid-flow works; landing is broken

**Surprise:** P16-N found customer entry at 48/100 overall but post-handoff at 83/100 and my-requests at 100/100. Blocker is 3-button category grid, not triage intelligence.

**Implication:** Deletion/reorder fixes customer entry without new capabilities.

---

## 9. product_only intentionally hides ~20 capabilities

**Surprise:** Lab surface hidden by design, not accident. Feels like "missing features" to engineers.

**Implication:** Don't re-add tabs to trial URL without cap contract justification.

---

## 10. Benchmark companies all do the same pipeline

**Surprise:** Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce, Stripe, Linear, HubSpot — different scale, identical chaos→case→next action architecture.

**Implication:** We built the right shape; we didn't deploy/discover it.

---

## 11. Chen Kui won't pay for software — he'll pay for time back

**Surprise:** Payment trigger is behavioral (one unsupervised draft to real client), not feature checklist.

**Implication:** Observation log and Day 0 matter more than Cap 4 customer tab.

---

## 12. Risk scores computed but never shown

**Surprise:** v4/v5 error/handoff risk on every triage — zero UI surfacing across all UI sprints.

**Implication:** Cheap trust win sitting in backend.

---

## 13. P16-Y fixed classification without draft regression

**Surprise:** +3.0 intelligence score, 0 regressions on office actionability — rules path sufficient for pilot.

**Implication:** CI battery gate is the right quality mechanism.

---

## 14. Demo queue is Chen Kui's #1 wrong-first-click

**Surprise:** P16-M Day-0 simulation showed 快速体验 competes visually with 开始整理 on first visit.

**Implication:** Demotion, not deletion — demo stays in contract.

---

## 15. Multi-turn is the real office workflow — and it's broken at summary merge

**Surprise:** Y44 correction case dropped turn 1 from summary. ~90% single-turn good; multi-turn forces re-read WeChat.

**Implication:** P16-Y P0 is the highest-impact engine fix remaining.

---

## 16. Customers interact through broker's WeChat in weeks 1–2, not our URL

**Surprise:** Customer-facing tab is week 3 asset, not Day 0 blocker — contradicts earlier customer-first assumptions.

**Implication:** Broker paste path is the entire GTM for pilot.

---

## 17. SimulationAssistant.tsx is orphan dead code

**Surprise:** Duplicate of ScenarioReplayTab with zero usage — created confusion across role simulation sprints.

**Implication:** Delete, don't maintain.

---

## 18. 10+ add-car battery scripts overlap

**Surprise:** Operator path has triple demo pack runners and redundant batteries while canonical guardrail exists.

**Implication:** Archive noise; one validation path.

---

## 19. First payment probability jumps 12% → 45% post-Day 7

**Surprise:** Not linear with feature count — gated entirely on supervised trial evidence.

**Implication:** Run Day 0, not build Cap 4.

---

## 20. WeChat beats the product unless first case is faster than manual draft

**Surprise:**「直接回微信」is the real competitor, not other SaaS. Under 45 seconds paste-to-copy is the bar.

**Implication:** Speed + Chinese specificity + append continuity = retention.

---

*End of P16-Z2.5 Top 20 Discoveries*
