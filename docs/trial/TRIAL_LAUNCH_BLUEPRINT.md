# Trial Launch Blueprint — Launch-Ready State

**Sprint:** Trial Launch + Fix-Now Queue Sprint  
**Created:** 2026-03-18  
**Purpose:** Explain why launch-readiness matters now, what this sprint strengthens, and what it intentionally will not do.

---

## 1. Why Launch-Readiness Matters Now

The product has evolved from demo → sellable package → scenario hardening → Real Broker Trial Package → Trial Execution Readiness. The trial package is documented and coherent. The **remaining gap** is that it is "ready in theory" but not yet "ready to launch in practice."

**Current state:**
- Trial package exists (docs/trial/)
- 5 core scenarios defined; Simulation Assistant + founder demo queue work
- Broker workflow, observation log, founder notes exist
- Guardrails pass; product is strong

**Gap:** A founder may still stumble when actually launching. A broker may not know exactly what to do on Day 1. Trial observations may not convert into a clear fix-now queue. There is no single "launch trial now" entry point.

---

## 2. Why This Is the Right Move After Trial Execution Readiness

| Prior step | Outcome |
|------------|---------|
| Trial Execution Readiness Sprint | Last-mile hardening, handoff spec, observation-to-iteration spec |
| Real Broker Trial Package | Blueprint, scope, scenario pack, workflow, metrics |

**Next logical step:** **Trial Launch + Fix-Now Queue** — push from "good trial package" toward "founder can launch with very low confusion" and "post-trial learning is immediately actionable."

---

## 3. What This Sprint Will Strengthen

1. **Single launch entry point** — One command or one-page checklist to launch the trial
2. **Founder launch clarity** — What to do immediately before first broker trial; no confusion
3. **Broker Day 1 flow** — Clear, minimal steps; broker understands what to do
4. **Evidence capture** — Structured trial log; useful evidence packet after 1 week
5. **Fix-now queue** — Ready-made structure for post-trial: fix now / fix next / defer
6. **1–2 small friction reductions** — Product or workflow tweaks that help during real trial

---

## 4. What This Sprint Will NOT Do

- Add broad new platform work
- Start large integrations (inbox sync, OCR, carrier API)
- Scatter across random feature ideas
- Make the product production-perfect
- Add Stripe, multi-tenant, auth

---

## 5. Success Definition

At the end of this sprint:
- Founder can launch the trial with very low confusion
- Broker can understand what to do on Day 1
- Office-side can review cases with less reconstruction
- Trial evidence gets captured in a more structured way
- There is a ready-made fix-now / fix-next / defer queue for immediate post-trial iteration
- At least one or two small friction points are reduced
