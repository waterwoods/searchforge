Financial Advisor / Financial Specialist

We are seeking a motivated Financial Advisor to join our wealth management team. 
The ideal candidate will be responsible for building and maintaining client relationships, 
prospecting new clients, and providing comprehensive financial planning services.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and maintain client relationships through proactive outreach and prospecting
- Provide financial planning services including retirement planning, estate planning, and investment advice
- Sell financial products including mutual funds, stocks, bonds, and insurance products
- Generate new business through referrals and networking
- Meet sales targets and revenue goals
- Obtain necessary licenses (Series 7, Series 63, CFP preferred)

Requirements:
- Strong sales and relationship management skills
- Experience in financial services or wealth management
- Excellent communication and client-facing abilities
- Ability to work independently and meet sales quotas
- Licensed or willing to obtain Series 7 and Series 63 licenses

Compensation:
- Base salary plus commission structure
- Performance-based bonuses
- Comprehensive benefits package

This is a sales-oriented position focused on client acquisition and revenue generation.
